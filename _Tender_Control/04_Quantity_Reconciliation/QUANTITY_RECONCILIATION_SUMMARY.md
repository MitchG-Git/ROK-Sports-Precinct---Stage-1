## Rules I could not apply cleanly

1. **Rule 2 — RCP375 "~+35 m":** the +35 m characterisation comes from the take-off note's design figure of ≈690.4 m, which under-sums the printed long-section chainages by ~24.5 m (line 06 starts at ch −24.498 and that leg appears dropped). Against the printed run-by-run sums the independent total is **714.9 m combined** and the true overage is **~+10.6 m**. I honoured the rule's direction (printed design data governs) and adopted CL3 702.9 m / CL4 12.0 m, with the discrepancy flagged as Opens #3.
2. **Rule 5 — NBC DP stub count:** the instruction's "19+ roof DPs on NBC-103" conflicts with the vol06_11 dedicated review (11 roof callouts / 12 ground labels). Adopted **19** (higher/conservative) at Low confidence with an RFI to settle the count (Opens #23).
3. **Rule 6 — never-counted wet areas:** there is no second *count* to reconcile for AR-NBC-3006–3009 — the fixture-count note explicitly bars its label-census numbers from use as quantities. I adopted the **hydraulic-sheet drawn counts** as interim conservative quantities at Low confidence rather than leaving ADOPTED blank; Phase 4 recount governs final numbers.
