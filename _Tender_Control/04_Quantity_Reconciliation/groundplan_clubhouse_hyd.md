## 4. Reconciliation

- **Legend vs mark-up pages:** the overlays visually match the legend layers (green sewer runs with fitting ticks on D/E; blue-dashed 110 HDPE trade-waste web + orange GIT square on F; blue/orange/green water runs with dense PEX fitting marks on H/I/J). No printed page subtotals or grand totals exist on the drawings to reconcile against — the legend export **is** the only stated total per plan, so legend-vs-page reconciliation is identically satisfied. No conflicts found between legend pages of the same plan.
- **Internal cross-checks:** the trade-waste vent stub (1.98 m + 2 fittings) sits on the Vent layer but is 110 HDPE — rolled into the trade-waste package in §3.3, not DWV. Items are identically named across the two layers, so any roll-up by item name merges them automatically (correct — count once). F's "DWV BEND 50 X 85 Deg 1 EA" inside the Trade Waste table (Geberit 50 connection) kept in trade waste.
- **Duplicate name caution:** "basket trap" 12 EA on the Sewer layer = floor-waste basket traps; "BASKET TRAP" 2 EA on J's Water layer = water charge/self-priming feed points to baskets. **Different scopes despite the same name — do not merge.**
- **MEASURED vs ANNOTATION:** all §2/§3 quantities are measured Groundplan objects. Drawing-native text (pipe labels, Rheem model callouts, IL levels 34.040–34.895, drawing notes, WC/BSN/SHR fixture tags, DP/VP hexagons, eaves-gutter "32000 mm² ECS" notes) is annotation and was NOT counted as quantity. The estimator did not itemise fixture connections as fixtures; his proxies are basket traps, 4-way risers, clear outs, IOS, tundishes and TMV counts.
- **Testing items:** none measured in any layer. Testing obligations exist only as drawing notes (NBC-002 note G4: 1-hour static water test + drainage test certificate with third-party-signed photographic evidence; G16: as-constructed drawings). Price from the notes, not this take-off.

The 2 EA on plan H could be two units near MECH-01/Amenities or a double-count — cannot resolve from the plan render. Verify unit count against the schedule before pricing 3 units.
2. **Hose tap on NBC-201 (plan I):** the drawing shows an HT symbol (top-right, near Officials Room), but I's legend has no hose-tap row — possible 1 EA undercount (set total would be 5, not 4).

6. **Dense fitting recounts:** symbol clusters (e.g. 68× bend 100x90, 71× junction on NBC-100) cannot be independently re-counted mark-by-mark at render resolution; legend totals accepted as the estimator's measurement of record (they are machine-generated from the mark-up objects).

## 6. Anomalies / flags for the estimating review

1. **No take-off for sheet 25015-H-NBC-103 (roof stormwater).** File G has no `-legend` export and no measured layers. Ø150 DPs (approx. 19+ shown), 200(W)x170(D) eaves gutters (2 runs noted at 32000 mm² ECS), and roof VP terminations are unquantified in this set. The 9 "already measured in civil sw" markers cover ground-level DP connection points only. **Confirm the civil SW take-off actually includes DP verticals, gutters and roof-level items, or they are missed entirely.**
2. **No testing/commissioning items** in any legend (drawing note G4 requires a static water test + certificate). Must be priced as an allowance.
