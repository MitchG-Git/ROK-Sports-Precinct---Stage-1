## 4. Reconciliation

- **Legend vs mark-up pages:** the mark-up overlays visually match the legend layers (green sewer runs with fitting ticks on D/E; blue-dashed 110 HDPE trade waste web + orange square at the GIT on F; blue/orange/green water runs with dense PEX fitting marks on H/I/J). No printed page subtotals or grand totals exist on the drawings to reconcile against — the legend export **is** the only stated total per plan. No conflicts found between legend pages of the same plan.
- **Internal cross-checks:** trade-waste vent stub (1.98 m + 2 fittings) sits on the Vent layer but is 110 HDPE — added to the trade-waste package above, not DWV. F's "DWV BEND 50 X 85 Deg 1 EA" appears in the Trade Waste table (Geberit 50 connection) — kept in trade waste.
- **MEASURED vs ANNOTATION:** all quantities in Section 2/3 are measured Groundplan objects. Drawing-native text (pipe labels, Rheem model callouts, IL levels, drawing notes, WC/BSN/SHR fixture tags, DP/VP hexagons) is annotation and was NOT counted as quantity. Fixture connection counts were not itemised by the estimator as fixtures; his proxies are basket traps, 4-way risers, clear outs, IOS and TMV counts.

## 5. Could not verify / ambiguities (recorded, not guessed)

1. **Rheem HWU = 3 EA total (H legend 2, J legend 1).** Drawings NBC-200 and NBC-202 each show one Rheem 315L callout (2 locations visible). The 2 EA on plan H could be two units at the MECH-01 location or a double-count — cannot resolve from plan view. Verify against mechanical/electrical schedule before pricing 3 units.
2. **Hose taps:** NBC-201 (plan I) shows an HT symbol top-right, but the I legend has no hose-tap row — possible 1 EA undercount (set total would be 5, not 4).
3. **RPZDs on NBC-202:** drawing shows RPZD symbols at ice-maker/SK area, storage, GIT wash-down and binwash (~4 positions); J legend records 3 EA (20mm) — possible 1 EA undercount, or one position is the 100mm check-valve/other device. Illegible at rendered resolution.
4. **Vent riser verticals:** measured vent totals (17.06 m of 100mm, 0.70 m of 50mm) can only be horizontal plan runs. The drawings show ~13+ vertical VP risers (Ø50/Ø75/Ø100) terminating 300 mm above roof; vertical heights are not measurable in plan and are NOT in these totals.
5. **Exact overlay-line lengths on mark-up pages** cannot be independently re-measured from the raster render; legend totals accepted as the estimator's measurement of record.

## 6. Anomalies / flags for the estimating review

1. **No take-off for sheet 25015-H-NBC-103 (roof stormwater).** File G has no `-legend` export and no measured layers. Ø150 DPs (approx. 19+ shown), 200x170 eaves gutters (2 runs noted at 32000 mm2 ECS), and roof VP terminations are unquantified in this set. The 9 "already measured in civil sw" markers cover ground-level DP connections only. **Confirm the civil SW take-off actually includes DP verticals, gutters and roof-level items, or they are missed entirely.**
2. **No testing/commissioning items** in any legend (task brief expected them; drawing note G4 requires static water test + certificate). Must be priced as a lump allowance.
3. **Spec deviation risk — WS2:** drawings require pipes > DN22 to be **316 stainless steel** with press-fit couplings; Denis measured **copper tube type B** for 25/32 mm (130.58 m combined) and B-press copper fittings. Material substitution needs pricing/qualification.
4. **Rev status:** all 12 sheets confirmed **T1 (Issue for Tender, 17.06.2026)** — nothing at any other revision. Titleblock page-numbering duplication ("10 of 10" on -202, -300 and -301) is an HKS drafting quirk only.
5. Legend pages carry empty repeated section headers (layer name printed twice, second table empty) — Groundplan export artifact, no data loss.
6. **Continuations out of set:** sanitary and water runs continue to site drawing 25015-H003 (binwash, GIT, incoming water) — those metres are outside this clubhouse set; ensure the site/civil hydraulic take-off picks them up (boundary = "FOR CONTINUATION" tags on D/F/H/J).
7. Minor naming inconsistencies in legend items ("BUCKEY TRAP", "ISOLATIVE VALVE", "Git Viking Panel TIm" = Viking PanelTim GIT, "COFFE MACHINE") — transcribed as printed.
