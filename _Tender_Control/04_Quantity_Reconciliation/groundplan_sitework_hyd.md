## 6. Anomalies / flags for reconciliation

1. **Rising main omitted** (see Could-not-verify 1). If the rising main is contractor scope (only the pump station itself is Council-installed), add ~60–110 m of Ø100 PE pressure pipe + fittings. If it is Council scope with the station, document that assumption.
2. **Size mapping vs base drawing:** runs the base drawing labels Ø20 are taken off as 25mm PE (74.25 m), and runs labelled Ø25 as 32mm PE (37.26 m). No Ø20 item exists in the take-off. Consistent one-size upsizing — confirm intent.
3. **Material call on the Ø200 main:** measured as copper only (Kembla Type B, 134.63 m) — resolves the "copper/HDPE ~135 m" claim to copper; no HDPE alternative was measured. Note copper Ø200 is a high-cost call vs DICL/PE for an in-ground authority-side main — worth a pricing review.
4. **Fire/CW spine sizing:** drawing annotates the spine "Ø100 FS / Ø100 CW INGROUND"; take-off uses 125 OD PE (PN16/SDR11) with 125PE x 4" MaxiStubs — correct OD-for-DN substitution for PE, internally consistent (12 stubs = 6 flanged valve sets).
5. **Storm scope:** only 6.91 m of Ø225 (10 stubs) + 10 bends measured; the long PVC-U DWV 225Ø @1:100 storm runs themselves are NOT taken off — presumably civil scope. Confirm the split so the 225 runs aren't lost between trades.
6. **QCPS32VC/A01 & A02 are Rev A "FOR APPROVAL" (27.03.2024)** vendor sample sheets, not T1 — expected for vendor drawings, and nothing is measured on them (pump station excluded — Council installs). All four HK sheets (H001–H003, H300) confirmed Rev T1 Tender Issue.
7. **Trade waste legend group labelled "Rev 1"** rather than "Rev T1" — cosmetic Groundplan naming only.
8. Only **1 dual pillar hydrant** on the whole site take-off (fire main otherwise ends at the booster) — matches the drawing, but flag for fire-coverage/BOQ cross-check against building packages.
9. The legend's Water group lists **"100mm fire water meter assembly and 100mm cold water water assembly"** as a single 1 EA item covering both assemblies — price as two assemblies under one item.
