Every page was scanned programmatically for coloured fills, and every hit inspected visually at 4–6x zoom. The colour census found exactly **6 red + 5 orange + 2 blue + 1 purple = 14 symbols on p2** and **8 orange + 2 yellow-green + 1 red = 11 symbols on p5**, and **zero Groundplan symbol fills on all other pages**. The salmon (#E96A54) shapes on all pages are title-block/key-plan graphics; brown/mint fills and the magenta dashed linework on p6–p9 are architectural drawing content (see §6 item 5) — none are mark-ups. Mark-ups exist **only** on p2 and p5, consistent with Groundplan exporting legends only for those two pages.
