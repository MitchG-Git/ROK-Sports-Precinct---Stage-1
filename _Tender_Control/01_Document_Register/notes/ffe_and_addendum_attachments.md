### Att 14 — Vegetation & Fauna Management Plan (site constraints for civil/TCS)

- Site: Lot 70 on SP304746, 7.73 ha (77,300 m2), RRC LGA, within CQU Rockhampton PDA (assessed under ED Act 2012, not Planning Act) (VFMP00/01).
- Regulated vegetation: Category X + Category C (High Value Regrowth); mapped Essential Habitat for Koala, Squatter Pigeon (southern) and Cycas ophiolitica; MLES Biodiversity Areas overlay (RE 11.12.1 & 11.3.4) (VFMP01).
- **Directional clearing mandatory: from the southern boundary northward** (access via existing track SW of site), pushing fauna toward retained habitat north incl. Limestone Creek Conservation Park (VFMP02 s1.0).
- **Temporary fauna exclusion fencing** along the eastern boundary (Norman Road interface) and western boundary (CQU developed areas); fauna-friendly; maintain throughout clearing + construction (VFMP02, locations on VFMP08).
- **DETSI-qualified Fauna Spotter Catcher (FSC)**: pre-clearance survey ~72 hrs prior; FSC present on site during ALL clearing; clearing prohibited until pre-clearance report accepted by relevant authority; post-clearance report required (VFMP02/03).
- **Machinery use prohibited 6pm-6am** unless superseding authority granted (VFMP02, Table 3).
- Clearing staging (VFMP04 s1.0): Stage 1 FSC survey 48 hrs before Council pre-start meeting; Stage 2 tree protection installed and approved before pre-start; Stage 3 Council pre-start within 48 hrs of fauna pre-clear, FSC must attend; Stage 4 fauna procedures; Stage 5 clearing.
- **Tree protection (VFMP04 s2.0)**: NRZ/TPZ per AS4970-2025 (TPZ = DBH(mm)/1000 x 12, min 1000mm per VFMP08 inset); fencing at NRZ boundary (1800mm steel posts ~1m spacing, HDPE barrier mesh); mulch mound 4m past NRZ if fencing hazardous; signage "Vegetation Protection - No Go Zone"; no excavation within drip line unless supervised by AQF Level 5 Project Arborist; no material storage within 1m of NRZ; compaction remediation by hand aeration to 75mm.
- **Works within NRZ (VFMP04 s2.1)**: earthworks excluded from Structural Root Zone; all activity supervised by AQF5 arborist; **any below-ground incursion within the NRZ must be water excavated under low pressure** under arborist supervision; pause works if roots >50mm dia encountered outside NRZ; post-works arborist audit report.
- **Trenching near retained trees (VFMP04 s4.0)** — directly relevant to TCS/TPS service trenching: trim damaged roots with clean cut; apply permeable antifungal agent to cut roots before backfilling; do NOT cover trench with hardened surface or concrete; backfill ASAP with existing soil; **root guards should be installed if retained trees are close to power cables, sewer/service lines** requiring excavation or maintenance; 100mm settled mulch where large NRZ areas cut.
- Hollow-bearing trees soft-felled (harvester head/crane methods preferred); stockpiled cleared vegetation held >24 hrs requires FSC inspection before mulching; Koala in tree = 10m radius work stoppage until it leaves (VFMP03/04).
- Weeds: treat prohibited/restricted pest plants before clearing; licensed commercial operator for chemical control; do not mulch weed species (Lantana, Leucaena present in dense patches); swale drains noted as ideal weed colonisation locations - restrict ground disturbance (VFMP04 s5.0).
- Erosion/sediment: comply IECA 2008 Best Practice + RRC standards as minimum; construction-phase ESCP recommended before clearing (VFMP04 s6.0).
- Machinery/vehicle washdown before entering site (VFMP10 Table 9).
- Imported fill to be clean and free of vegetative matter (VFMP10).
- Tree protection fence for individually retained trees: min 1.2m 3-wire, within 500mm of construction activity, sign every 100m "NO GO ZONE - VEGETATION PROTECTION AREA" red on white, 50mm letters (VFMP10 Table 9).
