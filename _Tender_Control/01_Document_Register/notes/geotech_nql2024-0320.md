## Obligations on us (as trench/earthworks subcontractor, TCS & TPS)

- **Excavatability (§5.3.3 p.19, exact wording):** TC-bit limit ≈ limit of a medium dozer (Cat D7E, D8) in bulk or a **large excavator (Kato or Hymac) in trench excavation**; "'TC' bit refusal (also indicated by SPT effective refusal N*>50 in wash bored locations) would generally require **larger excavation equipment, explosives, compressor driven pneumatic tools, or hydraulic rock breakers** for excavation." CONFIRMED verbatim.
- **Temporary batters (§5.3.3 p.19):** "Short term temporary batter slopes no steeper than **1.5H:1V** … during the construction phase **for excavations having depths of less than 2m** at the site. If a perched groundwater or soft material is encountered the stable slope would be approximately half this value (**3H:1V**), or alternatively benched, following inspections from a suitably qualified geotechnical engineer." NOTE: prior reviewer paraphrased "below 2m depth"; the document actually frames 1.5H:1V for excavations **less than 2 m deep** and gives **no batter recommendation for deeper excavations** (only benching/inspection) — deeper trenches by inference need shoring or engineered batters.
- **Trench entry (§5.3.3 p.19):** "Should trench excavations exceed **1.5m vertical bench height**, CMW recommends that a **qualified geotechnical engineer conduct a trench inspection** to deem the excavation satisfactory for personnel entry." CONFIRMED.
- Excavations: minimise open duration (moisture softening / "possible retrogressive collapse"); handle wet-season inflows by **pumping from sumps**; keep stockpiles/plant surcharge back at least the excavation depth from the crest (§5.3.3 p.19).
- Site preparation: strip grass/root-affected soil (~0.4 m, locally deeper); stripped topsoil not structural fill (may stockpile for landscaping); **existing fill is 'uncontrolled' — remove and recompact** (else deep footings + consult CMW); proof-roll with ≥10-t vibrating padfoot roller; remove/replace soft-loose zones; **MRTS04 within road boundaries, AS3798 elsewhere** (§5.3.1 p.18).
- Structural fill: all site materials **except topsoil and high-plasticity clay** acceptable with pre-treatment; clay fill in ≤200 mm loose layers, moisture −2%/+3% of OMC, **≥95% standard MDD (AS1289.5.1.1)**; keep clay fill from drying out; **Level 1 (AS3798) inspection/testing/certification**; bench fill into slopes >8H:1V, steps ≤0.5 m, free-draining (§5.3.2 p.18).
- Footing excavations: no prolonged open time; blinding layer unless cast immediately; geotechnical engineer to inspect all foundation excavations; clean, dry, free of loose/softened material (§5.5.1 p.21, §5.5.2 p.21).
- Retaining structures: clean granular backfill, drainage/weep holes, design water pressure to at least half wall height, only hand compaction directly behind walls (§5.4.2 p.20).
- Pavement works: clearing/grubbing + separate topsoil stockpiling without contamination; proof roll; remove/replace soft spots; erosion & sediment control maintained; drainage of working areas without ponding; subsoil drainage to limit pavement moisture ingress (§5.6 p.23).
- Report re-use caveat: prepared for SPORTENG; other parties to seek CMW consent; recommendations require confirmation during construction; retain geotechnical services through construction (§6.0 p.25, "Using your CMW report" p.26).

## Conflicts & discrepancies

1. **"Collapsible sand" (Addendum 7 Q3) — NOT FOUND.** The phrase "collapsible sand" (or "collapsible" at all) does not appear anywhere in the report text or logs. Closest wording: §5.3.3 p.19 "the subsoils would be susceptible to moisture softening and possible **retrogressive collapse**" (re groundwater inflow into excavations) and §5.5.2 p.21 "pile excavations may be susceptible to **collapse**". Loose/uncontrolled sandy FILL exists (TP04 0–1.8 m; TP03 0–0.2 m) and MD sands occur (BH04 2.0–2.5 m, BH07/TP07 sand layers), but the geotech report never uses the term Addendum 7 Q3 attributes to it.
2. **TP/CBR10 termination:** Table 1 (p.10) "Equipment Refusal" at 1.10 m vs log (p.143) Termination Reason "Target Depth". Unresolved in document.
3. **BH01 termination depth:** 6.45 m (Table 1 p.9; log p.30) vs 6.50 m (Table 4 p.13).
4. **Steel aggressivity basis:** Table 14 (p.24) adopts AS2159 **Soil Condition B** only. ALS certs (pp.180, 183) show **Condition A (sands in groundwater): TP07 steel SEVERE; BH04/BH07/BH12 Moderate; concrete Mild**. Given §4.2's expectation of wet-season perched groundwater and sand layers (e.g. TP07 sand 1.8–3.0 m is the very sample), buried steel below seasonal water could see a harsher class than the report's headline. Report body does not disclose this.
5. **Excavator size inconsistency:** §1.2 (p.6) says test pits by "**14-tonne excavator**"; §3.2 (p.9) says "**5 tonne excavator**"; logs record 5-t (Jun mobilisation), Hitachi 12-t (Sep), and **HITACHI 30-ton for TP/CBR04 only** (p.124). Refusal depths are therefore not machine-consistent: most refusals were logged with 5–12-t machines, and the one pit dug with a 30-t machine reached target. Direct bearing on excavatability/rock-allowance pricing.
6. **Table 7 CBR summary incomplete:** lists 5 of 8 soaked CBRs; TP/CBR04 (2.5%), TP/CBR08 (6%), TP/CBR09 (2.5%) exist only in Appendix C (pp.175–177). §5.6's design CBR 1.5% predates/ignores the (better) Sept results — conservative but check against pavement design docs.

8. §4.2 p.15 editorial: "Whilst no groundwater was not observed within other locations" (double negative; intent clear).
9. Table 8 is captioned "Table 81", Table 9 "Table 92", Figure 6 "Figure 66" (pp.16–17, 25) — OCR/typo artefacts in source document, cross-references remain unambiguous.
