## Conflicts & discrepancies

1. **Cover date vs rev table:** cover says "May 2026" but T1 Tender Issue is 17.06.2026 (PDF p1-2).
2. **Council name:** 3.3 table (p5/17), 5.1.15 (p37/49) and 9.21 (p63/75) say "Rockhampton City Council"; O&M Segment 5(d) (p34/46) says "Rockhampton Regional Council". Actual authority is Rockhampton Regional Council (Fitzroy River Water is its water business). Spec's authority table says "Fitzroy Water" - consistent with FRW, **no SEQWater/Urban Utilities reference anywhere in the full 153-page text (grep-verified)**.
3. **NSW template leftovers (QLD project):** standards table lists "EP&A - Environmental Planning and Assessment Act 1979" (NSW Act) (3.3, p5/17); 3.52 Food premises requires compliance with "the New South Wales Health Department" (p22/34); 5.1.10 & 8.11 invoke the NSW "Building Professionals Board (BPB) Act" and "PCA" inspection of the stormwater connection point (p36/48, p58/70); 3.3 also leaves in an internal designer note: "The designer is to check the gas standard in the table below...".
4. **9.22 Joint sewer (p63/75):** states an existing joint sewer + boundary trap serving several upstream properties exists on site, exact location unknown, to be located, re-laid and 150mm concrete encased under the new building. Almost certainly a template leftover for this greenfield precinct, but it is a live obligation as written - RFI before pricing.
5. **Non-project template clauses:** 9.15 swimming pool backwash; 9.24 bio-septic coordination; 3.33 protection of ground floor shops; 3.31 keeping existing building operational/staged changeovers; 3.55 carpark head heights; 5.4 electrical table full of "?" placeholders, gas heaters, science rooms/laboratories, carpark exhaust, reverse osmosis, rainwater re-use, oil arrestor; 5.1.9 item 31 "Hydrant Tank on level?? including water proofing" (incomplete text with "??"); 12.18 fire control centre for buildings over 50m (TOC). None obviously apply to two single-level buildings - clarify scope inclusion.
6. **3.58 conflict rule:** any doc conflict = deemed to have priced the larger quantity / more expensive component, and spec priority over drawings - lump-sum risk amplifier; ambiguities must be notified at tender time to preserve position.
7. **Compaction inconsistency:** 7.8.1 (p53-54/65-66) requires backfill "not less than 95%... Standard Compaction" generally, while 3.62 schedule requires 93% Modified (buildings, non-vehicular), 95% Modified (vehicular), 98% Modified (pits/pump stations). Source tables also internally typo-ridden ("MOD"/"MDD", "every 2\" layer", "peer 50 metres").
8. **Water-charged ground responsibility split:** 5.1.1(4) (p35/47) says the ground IS water charged (per geotech) and sewer pipes in water-charged/unstable ground require a structural support solution designed and certified by a structural engineer - listed under BUILDING CONTRACTOR works; but 7.7 (p53/65) makes the plumbing contractor's bedding for water-charged ground "deemed to be included in the subcontract tender", and 7.10 has the plumbing contractor allowing for the Structural Engineer for filled-ground support. Boundary of design/cert cost is ambiguous - RFI.
9. **Detention tank overlap:** 5.1.12 (p36/48) builder constructs/sizes detention pit + surveyor certification, but 8.13 (p58/71) plumbing contractor supplies grates, step rungs, flap valve, orifice plate, debris screen - interface must be priced precisely. (Also check against civil/WSUD scope - possible overlap with TCS.)
10. **Downpipes:** 8.3 (p57/69) visible downpipes = ACO SS 316 supplied/installed by BUILDING CONTRACTOR, exposed downpipes by roofer, but internal/cast-in HDPE downpipes = Plumbing Contractor - scope table must be mirrored in our exclusions.
11. **Fire Authority named generically** "Fire and Rescue Of the state" and QFD padlock "003" in 5.7 - QFES/QFD naming inconsistent but functionally QLD.
12. **1.1 vs drawing list:** 1.1 lists no gas scope, yet Gas Authority (Origin), AS 5601, gas warning tape, gas fittings clauses persist throughout - likely no gas in Stage 1; confirm before pricing gas-related items.
