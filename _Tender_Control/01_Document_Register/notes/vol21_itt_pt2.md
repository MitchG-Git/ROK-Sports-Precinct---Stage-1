## Conflicts & discrepancies

1. **Plumbing approval vs FRW split (S16, p9):** Plumbing Subcontractor lodges plumbing applications with RRC and pays fees; Principal Contractor lodges FRW Private Works Applications and completes water/sewer connection works. TPS must NOT assume FRW application lodgement/fees — but Paynters may try to flow the FRW obligation down. Scope-split must be nailed in TPS tender qualifications.
2. **Trade Waste is not mentioned anywhere in this document.** The clubhouse includes a kiosk/canteen "(if specified)" (S4.2) which would normally trigger an FRW trade waste application/pre-treatment (grease arrestor). Silent — RFI required.
3. **No revision/date inside VOL 21** — cannot confirm we hold the current issue; verify against Paynters transmittal/addenda.
4. **S16 fee reimbursement vs S18 preamble:** plumbing/FRW fees reimbursed "through the Provisional Sums allocated within the Contract", but this Part 2 nowhere states the Provisional Sum values — must verify Schedule 3 actually contains them; if absent, fees become a tenderer cost.
5. **S6 vs Schedule 3 cross-reference:** S6 directs pricing of mains connections to "Stage 1A Item 2.1 (Mains Connections)" and S7 directs Southern Section electrical to "Item 6.0" — these item numbers must be verified against the actual Schedule of Prices; misalignment between spec and schedule is a classic claims seed.
6. **S20.h program allowance of only 5 days for "inclement weather or a latent condition"** (combined!) for a Rockhampton project anticipated to commence 2027 (wet season exposure) — conflicts with realistic CQ weather expectations; a program-risk/qualification item. (Anticipated 2027 commencement is not stated in this document — it comes from other tender parts; no onsite works period stated here, "Program to be supplied by Contractor", S20 p14.)
7. **S25 lump sum / quantities-are-estimates** vs S18 schedule-of-rates provisions: rock, unsuitable material, over-depth piles and imported fill are remeasurable at scheduled rates only with prior written direction — everything else is lump sum risk on the tenderer.
8. **Head-contract wording used for a subcontract tender:** the document binds "The Contractor (The Principal Contractor)" throughout; TCS/TPS price as subcontractors to Paynters (folder 260701-Paynters). Which obligations Paynters flows down (e.g., ADAC as-cons, fauna spotter, rumble grids, temporary services) must be checked against Paynters' subcontract package — only the S16 Plumbing Approval clause names the Plumbing Subcontractor directly.
9. VOL 20 sketch is Rev B dated 12/06/26; S6 references "se_12306_skt_cmp" without a rev — assume Rev B is current, but confirm no later rev in Appendix Folder.
10. Water/Sewer authority confirmed as **Fitzroy River Water** (S16, p9) and governing standard confirmed **CMDG** (S22.1, p16) — consistent with tender basis, no SEQ references found. No conflict, recorded as verification.
