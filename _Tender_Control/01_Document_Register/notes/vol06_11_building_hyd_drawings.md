### INSTALLATION GUIDELINES FOR DRAINAGE SYSTEM INSTALLED IN REACTIVE SOIL
- For drainage installed in reactive soils, "STORM PLASTICS SWIVEL EXPANSION AND EXPANSION JOINTS" or equal approved flexible joints shall be installed.
- Where drainage is supported/bracketed to underside of building frames: mechanical flexible joints.
- Where drains are installed underside of concrete slab: combo joints for risers and Expanda joints on horizontal drains per AS/NZS 2032-2006 and manufacturer's recommendations.
- Where drains are installed outside the building: Expanda/swivel joints per AS/NZS 2032-2006; ensure adequate lagging to joints.
- **For downpipes continued and connected to in-ground stormwater pipes, provision for expansion and contraction at spaces not exceeding 6 metres.**
