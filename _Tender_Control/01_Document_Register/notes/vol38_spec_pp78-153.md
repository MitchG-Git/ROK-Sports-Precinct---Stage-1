## Conflicts & discrepancies
1. **Hydrant & hose reel "non-potable" colouring (§12.15, §13.11 - PDF 86, 89):** both clauses state the fire services are "connected to a Non-Potable system" and must be coloured Lilac P-23, yet §13.1 says the hose reel service connects to a *metered cold water service*, and the project water authority supply (Fitzroy River Water potable main) is potable. Almost certainly boilerplate from a recycled-water project. Query: is lilac colouring actually required? Affects material purchasing.
2. **§15 heading error (PDF 92):** Section 15 is titled "Extinguishers & fire blankets" but its content is the Reverse Osmosis service - duplicate of §14's title. Also RO service is highly unlikely on a netball precinct; yet "Reverse Osmosis Service" is a priced line in the §16 tender break-up. Query applicability.
3. **Extinguisher supply responsibility (§14.1 note vs §16):** spec instructs plumber to supply & install extinguishers AND prices them in the tender break-up, but the note says they are "generally installed by the Builder or Sprinkler contractor" and to "confirm with the client who is providing" them. Unresolved split - must be qualified in tender.
4. **VIC references in a QLD job:** "Fire and Rescue VIC Requirement" signage row (§21.8, PDF 111) and "VIC Health" temperature/Legionella testing rows (§26.6, PDF 143). QLD equivalents apply; template errors.
5. **"Refer to the schedule at the rear of the specification" (§10.9, §12.8, §15.5)** - §12.8 specifically defers the **booster valve and double check valve with metered by-pass** to "the rear of the specification", but pp 133-153 (the rear) contain only the checklist, maintenance tables, material change form and Appendix A cover. No equipment schedule exists in this half. Unless it is in pp 1-77 (unusual), the fire booster/DCDA and hot water plant schedules are MISSING from the spec - price from drawings and qualify.
6. **No hot water plant schedule** anywhere in pp 78-153 despite §11.1 requiring heaters and §16 pricing "Hot Water Plant and Pumps" - confirm with first-half note/drawings.
7. **Tender break-up template gaps (§16):** "Council Stormwater Pit (????)" left unresolved by the consultant; Gas Service line included (confirm any gas scope exists); grease trap sizes unselected; commercial kitchen locations marked "???" (§22.10-22.14).
8. **§26 maintenance tables include plant that does not exist on this project** (ozone laundry, Boilerland boilers, UV warm water disinfection, sprinklers, EWIS) - do not price recurring maintenance beyond the explicit 12-month hydrant/FHR obligation without clarification.
9. **§22.5/22.6 formatting corruption:** "Overflow Gully - Flood Prone Areas" heading appears twice with the swivel-joint table interleaved (PDF 114); §22.7 duplicates the Plastec 16365 with Material listed as "HDPE" vs "Plastic" in §22.6.
10. Sewer maintenance structures: spec nominates BOTH Humes precast concrete 600mm manholes (§22.43) and iPlex EZIpit plastic maintenance shafts (§22.44) with no rule for which applies where - drawings/CMDG/FRW requirements govern; FRW acceptance of EZIpit should be checked.
11. **No mention of Fitzroy River Water, CMDG or ADAC anywhere in pp 78-153.** Authority references are generic ("Local Authority", "Trade Waste Authority", "the water authority") plus QFD for fire. As-constructed requirement in §25 is limited to "As Built drawings" - **no ADAC data deliverable is specified in this half** (any ADAC/CCTV obligations must come from pp 1-77 or civil/authority docs).
12. §12.14 temporary fire protection is written for multi-storey construction (12m effective height, "highest poured concrete slab") - unlikely to apply to Stage 1A/1B buildings, but the clause is unconditional in its second paragraph ("Construct temporary fire protection during the construction process"). Qualify or seek clarification.
