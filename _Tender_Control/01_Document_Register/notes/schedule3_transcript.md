These are head-contract schedule lines; they flow down to TCS/TPS where our scope is engaged:
- **As-constructed drawings + ADAC XML files** as part of Quality Control/Approvals (1A R11, 1B R11) — ADAC deliverables are an RRC/CMDG requirement; TCS (stormwater) and TPS (water/sewer) as-constructed data will feed this.
- Environmental management incl. erosion & sediment control (1A R8, 1B R8) and Management Plans (1A R9, 1B R9).
- Traffic control (1A R7, 1B R7), setout survey (1A R10, 1B R10).
- Erection only of two (2) project signs, signs provided (1A R22).
- Rate-schedule obligations: rock and unsuitable-ground rates are to be priced "as defined in tender specification" (1A R141–142, 1B R116–117) — the definitions live in the spec, not here; those rates will govern trench excavation latent-condition recovery for TCS/TPS.
- Asphalt price-adjustment special condition requires declaring a tender-time asphalt supply price (1A R146–147, 1B R120–121) — not a TCS/TPS trade item but a tab-completeness requirement for the head tender.
- Connection fees carried as actual-cost-reimbursement provisional (1A R131, 1B R111) — implies the contractor pays authority fees and claims at cost; TPS should **exclude** Fitzroy River Water / RRC connection and application fees from base pricing.

## Conflicts & discrepancies

1. **Stage 1A Lump Sum Total formula omits Section 6.0.** F138 `=SUM(F6:F131)` excludes F134–F136 (Southern Section Site Works – Electrical) and the section Total F137. Any price entered against 6.1–6.3 does not roll up into the Lump Sum Total. (Refs: 1A R133–R138.)
2. **Double-count exposure in both roll-ups.** Both SUM ranges include section "Total" rows *and* line rows: 1A F138 range includes F14, F58, F83, F129; 1B F113 range includes F6–F12 (line placeholders) *plus* Totals F14, F37, F79. If a tenderer fills both levels, the lump sum double-counts. (Refs: 1A R14/58/83/129/138; 1B R6–14/37/79/113.)
