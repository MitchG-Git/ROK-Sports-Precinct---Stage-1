## 6. Penalties and constraints built into the rates (traceability)

- **Flat-grade laser penalty** (−15–20%) on RCP/PP mains — runs at 1:500 / 1:250 / 1:248 (vol15 obligation 1).
- **Rock-affected band** — separate allowance line A-08, not smeared into base rates (25/36 refusal, XW 0.2–2.2 m).
- **Shields/batters per geotech** — batters 1.5H:1V only for <2 m; entered excavations >1.5 m bench (WSUD, C10, deep sewer legs) carry shields + geotech inspection attendance.
- **VFMP** — arborist-supervised work near TPZs, **water excavation (vac truck) in NRZs**, 6pm–6am machinery curfew (no night recovery), washdown, fauna-spotter gates ahead of every new front.
- **No site power/water (ITT S17.3)** — gensets in both establishments; **temp potable water cartage + storage for all testing/flushing/disinfection** (TCS A-09, TPS T2-10/T1-11).
- **Meter-slab congestion** — +1 day inside T2-03 for the meters/RPZD/booster/bin-store/entry-sign cluster.
- **EDQ 20-day gates** — carried as discrete remobilisation allowance lines (TCS A-11, TPS T1-12), not spread.
- **Under-slab congestion + articulation** (H1 "P", bored piers/ground beams) — −30% on NBC/PAM UG drainage rates plus a separate articulation adder line.
- **Holidays/RDOs 2027 (assumption stated)** — ~12 QLD public holidays incl. Rockhampton Show Day plus industry RDOs absorbed within the 21-work-day month convention; **5-day weather/latent (S20.h) carried as terminal float, not in rates** — with a stated qualification that 5 days is unrealistic for a CQ wet season.
