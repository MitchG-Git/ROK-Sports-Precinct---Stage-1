# TENDER QUERY / RFI SCHEDULE — TEN16699 Rockhampton Sports Precinct Stage 1A & 1B
TriCore Civil Services (TCS) · TriCore Plumbing Services (TPS) · tender-run-01 · issued with both submissions

**Why this schedule exists — and why it is commercially mandatory.**
Hydraulic Specification VOL 38 §3.58 and drawing H002 note 15 both provide that where documents conflict or are ambiguous, the tender is **deemed to have priced the larger quantity or the more expensive component**, unless the ambiguity is raised at tender. This schedule raises them. Each query states the query, the documents in conflict, **the assumption we have adopted for pricing**, and the commercial consequence if the answer differs. Our price is based on the adopted assumptions stated below; where an answer alters the basis, we reserve the right to adjust.

Priority: **P1** = price-changing or scope-defining, answer needed before award · **P2** = material, manageable by qualification · **P3** = administrative/documentation.
